Circular Navigator — 0-to-1 SaaS Analytics Platform

Verisk Analytics Technical Product Manager 2018 – 2022

Took an acquired product from launch to $97M+ in revenue over 4 years. The acquisition gave Verisk the technology — but the technical PM doing the discovery, segmentation, and pricing work is what scaled it beyond a single-tier product for a narrow slice of the insurance market.

1 The Problem

Verisk acquired an Irish startup with a data product that tracked insurance-industry activity — speeding tickets, claims, regulatory changes — for insurers like USAA, AAA, and GEICO. The product had strong data but no product strategy: one pricing tier, one customer segment, and no clear path to scale.

The market opportunity was real but unaddressed: small-to-mid-market insurers were underserved by enterprise-grade analytics tools priced for Fortune 100 carriers. Vision: double customer adoption and grow revenue.

2 The Customers

  • Enterprise insurers — USAA, AAA, GEICO — needed deep data access and custom integrations.
  • Mid-market insurers — underserved by existing tools; needed the same insights at a lower price point with faster time-to-value.
  • Technical teams at insurer clients — wanted raw API access to pipe data into their own ecosystems, not just a UI.

Customers were paying full price for features they never used. Different customers had fundamentally different needs — but we were selling them the same product. I validated this with the data science team.

3 The Solution I Owned

I ran a two-phase discovery-to-launch process:

  • Problem discovery: competitive analysis to identify the SMB gap, plus customer interviews that surfaced two pain points — UI limitations slowing decisions, and pricing misaligned to actual usage.
  • Solution discovery: redesigned UI with a design workshop (wireframes, mocks, key customers, design and tech leads), and launched a three-tier subscription model based on usage-pattern segmentation.
  • Go-to-market: partnered with sales and marketing for insurance-industry conference promotion, channel sales to Verisk's existing customer base, and a one-month free trial.
  • Later pivot: identified the API-as-a-Service opportunity — opened direct API access for technical clients, creating an entirely new revenue stream.

4 Results & Measurement

In one year, customer accounts grew 2.8× (50 → 140) and revenue grew by 60%. Over four years, the product generated $97M+ in revenue.

Measurement framework

Adoption: new customers onboarded through each sales and marketing channel.

Conversion: customers moving from basic to premium tiers.

Churn: customers lost ÷ total customers.

CAC: sales & marketing cost ÷ new customers acquired.

Learning: Tiered pricing can cannibalize adjacent subscription products — worth accepting to reach a new segment, but build the migration path for overlapping customers up front.
