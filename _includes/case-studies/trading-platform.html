Trading Platform Modernization & Client Activation

Bank of America Sr. Technical Product Manager / Program Lead 2022 – Present

A modernization of a mission-critical trading platform handling 2M+ orders per hour. I owned specific customer journeys — authentication, order management, execution experience, APIs, and operational workflows. The migration could have been run as a pure engineering program — but the technical PM owning the customer-journey lens, prioritization, and cross-functional trade-offs is what made the difference between shipping infrastructure and delivering business outcomes.

1 The Problem

A legacy mainframe-based trading platform consumed 35% of engineering capacity in maintenance and was approaching end-of-life support — creating cyber exposure and blocking feature velocity. User-level churn was rising at 10%, driven by latency volatility during market spikes and a rigid UI/UX that slowed task completion.

Deployment cycles created constant tension between agility and regulatory safety — compliance required slower, more careful releases that frustrated product and business stakeholders.

2 The Customers

  • Institutional investors — hedge funds, asset managers, broker-dealers — executing high-frequency trades via FIX and REST APIs. Wanted self-service onboarding and real-time order visibility.
  • Retail partners — third-party brokerage platforms routing orders on behalf of end users. Needed reliable, low-latency integrations.
  • Internal teams — trading advisors, compliance, fraud analytics, and customer service — each a stakeholder in the platform's reliability and auditability.

3 The Solution I Owned

I owned specific customer journeys across the platform and ran a three-workstream strategy prioritized against business value and regulatory constraints:

  • Trading backend: built a sandbox + API Dev Portal for partners, cutting friction in test onboarding. Added telemetry to track partner errors and automate alerting. Led a UI redesign that introduced real-time log access with role-based visibility and contextual filters — cutting log retrieval time to under 60 seconds, helping reduce onboarding time by 75%.
  • Fraud detection integration: introduced an LLM-driven fraud scoring system — starting with a POC trained on historical triage logs. Worked with ML leads to define interpretability requirements to pass compliance scrutiny.
  • User interface and performance: shipped an interactive, customizable, widget-style UI for individual investors — 78% of adoption came from this work.
  • API-first modernization: decomposed legacy services into API-first microservices behind the partner-facing portal, and migrated key capabilities to AWS.
  • Self-service onboarding: automated validations across Operations, Compliance, and Technology that had previously been manual hand-offs.

I made the trade-offs: sequencing onboarding modernization first (fastest path to revenue), keeping the compliance requirements in scope, and keeping the UI redesign in scope when it would have been easier to cut.

4 Results & Measurement

Feature delivery time dropped by 60%, platform scalability now supports roughly 2 million orders per hour at market peak, and scope changes dropped by 50%. Client onboarding time fell from six weeks to under five days.

Measurement framework

Suggested success criteria would differ by audience — "external users" and "internal users" wanted different things:

External users — latency, uptime, self-service onboarding completion rate, API adoption.

Internal users — control, traceability, regulatory audit readiness, operational defect rate.

Business — feature delivery time, scope-change rate, client churn during migration.

Learning: Instrument partner-facing telemetry from day one. Partner pain points that surface in support tickets for months are the ones a real-time dashboard would have caught immediately.
