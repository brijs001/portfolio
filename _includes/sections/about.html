About me

Ambiguous problems in. Shipped products out.

I'm a technical product manager with a software engineering foundation. I started my career building systems for the NYC Department of Education and NYPD, moved into technical leadership at Verisk Analytics, and for the past four years have led product at Bank of America.

What I do best: take an ambiguous, technically complex problem — an agentic AI system, a legacy platform migration, an acquired product with no strategy — and turn it into a shipped product with measurable business impact. I write the PRDs, run the discovery, make the prioritization calls, and own the metrics.

I work at the intersection of AI/ML, cloud infrastructure, and B2B SaaS — industries where the technical decisions and the business decisions can't be separated.
